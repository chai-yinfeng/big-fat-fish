## Logo (`icon.svg`)

The sidebar logo and browser tab icon are a face crop of a community fan image.

- Image: "Q 版大肥鱼 · 三视图" from the 鲸鱼娘开源资产库 (TreapGoGo, community-maintained),
  https://github.com/TreapGoGo/deepseek-whale-girl, file
  `characters/dafeiyu/reference-sheets/dafeiyu-chibi-character-sheet.png`.
  Cropped to the face close-up and resized to 128×128, embedded as a PNG data URI.
- Character: 上善无形「溟月」 → ZipZipPipe 女仆装鲸鱼娘, shared for derivative works under
  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

  (it appears to be AI-generated from the site's published prompts).
